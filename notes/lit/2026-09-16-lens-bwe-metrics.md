# BWE literature lens: winning LSD and audio-ViSQOL cheaply

All claims below are from the fetched primary sources; items I could not verify are flagged.

## 1. SOTA on VCTK → 48 kHz (state the STFT basis!)

LSD bases differ: AP-BWE, AERO and NVSR use n_fft 2048 / hop 512 (NVSR hop 441), log10 ([AP-BWE](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.06387), [AERO](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2211.12232), [NVSR](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2203.14941)); NU-Wave 2 uses n_fft 1024 / hop 256 ([NU-Wave 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2206.08545v2)), i.e. the same basis as your 1024/256, so NU-Wave 2's numbers are the ones directly comparable to yours. ## 2. Loss-side tricks and their measured LSD effect

- **Low-band passthrough**: NVSR ablation 0.96 → 0.84 LSD ([NVSR](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2203.14941)); FlowHigh 12→48 LSD-LF 0.62 → 0.24 with STFT-domain replacement, LSD-HF unchanged (FlowHigh Tab. I). **Crossover design matters**: Vocos-BWE 8→48 no refiner 0.897, naive HP/LP 0.865, Butterworth 0.861, Linkwitz-Riley polynomial mask 0.850 ([Vocos-BWE](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.07285)).
- **Adversarial spectral critics are the largest single LSD lever in AP-BWE**: full 0.84 / ViSQOL 3.35; w/o MPD 0.85 / 3.29; w/o MRAD+MRPD 0.88 / 3.26; w/o all discriminators 1.50 / 3.26 (8→48). Phase metrics barely move, so the critics act on the amplitude stream ([AP-BWE Tab. VIII](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.06387)). Counter-evidence: AERO 8→16 without discriminator got the *best* LSD 0.879 and ViSQOL 3.363 but MUSHRA 32 vs 85.6 with 3 MSDs (LSD 0.943, ViSQOL 3.275) ([AERO Tab. 3](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2211.12232)). Waveform (MSD) critics hurt LSD; log-magnitude critics help it. APNet2's ablation: removing MRD raised LAS-RMSE 6.34 → 7.14 dB ([APNet2](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.11545v1)).
- **AP-BWE losses** (log base e): L_A = (1/TF) ||log(Y_a/Ŷ_a)||_F^2; phase L_IP, L_GD, L_IAF = (1/TF)||f_AW(·)||_1 with f_AW(x) = |x − 2π·round(x/2π)|; weights λ_A=45, λ_P=100, λ_C=45 (complex/consistency), adversarial hinge 1 (MPD) / 0.1 (MRD). ViSQOL is phase-insensitive ([ViSQOL v3](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2004.09584)) and LSD is magnitude-only, so phase losses only help these metrics indirectly.
- **Envelope + fine-structure split** ([LACE](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2307.06610), reused by Opus BWE): L_env = ||log X_s − log Ŷ_s||_1 on ERB-smoothed |STFT|; L_spec = 1 − Σ|X||Ŷ| / sqrt(Σ|X|² Σ|Ŷ|²) (scale-invariant spectral convergence); L_phase = ||x−ŷ||²/||ŷ|| (energy-preserving on uncorrelated parts); DFT sizes 2^n, n=5..12. Opus BWE pretrains with (1/13)L_env + (2/13)L_spec + (10/13)L_tdlp, the last an L2 on the 0–4 kHz band only, then a 2D-conv discriminator on log-magnitude spectrograms with frequency-positional embeddings ([2412.11392](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.11392)). No ablation numbers.
- **High-band-only spectral loss**: DDSP-BWE computes multi-scale L1 on |STFT| and log|STFT| (FFT 2048…64) "on the missing high-frequency region" only; 4.4K-param harmonic+noise model beats a 55K ResNet and SBR on LSD (5.68 vs 14.04 vs 9.27, dB scale, OrchideaSOL) and in MUSHRA ([DDSP-BWE](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.07363v3)).
- **LSD as a loss**: FSC-Net trains L = L_mr-stft + 5·L_lsd per progressive stage; progressive learning moved LSD 0.8857 → 0.8771, PESQ 2.70 → 2.81 ([FSC-Net](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.06962)).
- **Weightings that win**: BAE-Net L_G = 100·L_wav + 0.5·L_stft + L_adv + 10·L_feat ([BAE-Net](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2312.13722)); n-SBG λ_mel=15 across 7 mel resolutions ([n-SBG](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.06732)). Your 0.01 on the log-magnitude term is 2–4 orders of magnitude below all of these relative to the waveform term.

## 3. Architecture tricks at 88K

- **Snake**: AERO's 8→16 ablation: ReLU vs Snake within ±0.015 LSD and ±0.03 ViSQOL, "no significant improvement" from any single component ([AERO Tab. 4](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2211.12232)). BigVGAN (vocoder, not BWE): HiFi-GAN M-STFT 0.977 → +Snake 0.884 → +anti-aliased up/down (Kaiser sinc, 2× oversampled nonlinearity) 0.857; f_α(x) = x + (1/α) sin²(αx) ([BigVGAN](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2206.04658)). For your 11-sample receptive field, the anti-aliased nonlinearity is the only piece with mechanistic relevance (an implicit MLP evaluated at continuous time behaves like the "continuous signal cannot be represented" case BigVGAN describes).
- **Explicit amplitude/phase output**: AP-BWE; dropping A→P or P→A connection costs only 0.01 LSD.
- **Baseband envelope conditioning**: Opus BWE feeds a 32-band ERB log-magnitude spectrogram + phase differences of the first 40 bins through a GRU at 100 Hz ([2412.11392](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.11392)); BAE-Net's 128-band ERB band-guided mask ablation: 8→48 SegSNR 26.15 → 21.23 dB when removed.
- **Folded / nonlinear excitation**: Opus BWE generates the band above 8 kHz with f(x) = x·sin(log|x|) (voiced) and AdaShape, "spectral folding via adaptive weighting" (unvoiced), then adaptive pre/post filters; model inspection shows AdaShape dominates unvoiced, the nonlinearity voiced. Makhoul & Berouti's folding/translation and rectification are summarised in [1602.08215](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1602.08215): folding "creates artificial tones", rectification "preserves the harmonic structure" while adding inter-harmonic noise. Nagel & Disch (ICASSP 2009) claim copy-up causes "unpleasant roughness, especially for tonal items", fixed by phase-vocoder harmonic transposition (abstract only: [ACM DL](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1109/ICASSP.2009.4959541); full text 403). For a speech band above 6 kHz that you measured as ≤2 % coherent, roughness is a non-issue; the incoherent band is fricative noise where SBR-style noise + envelope is the classical answer.
- **Sub-band domain**: UBGAN synthesises the upper 4 of 8 PQMF sub-bands with a 250K causal generator, pretrained on spectral-convergence + log-magnitude losses, then LSGAN over four STFT discriminators (2048…256) ([UBGAN](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.16404)); mdctGAN uses arcsinh-compressed MDCT with adversarial + feature-matching losses only.

## 4. ViSQOL audio mode

Pipeline: 48 kHz, gammatone spectrogram with 32 bands (speech mode: 16 kHz, 21 bands, VAD), ~0.5 s patches, global then patch-wise alignment, NSIM per band averaged, SVR to MOS-LQO capped at ~4.75; NSIM is computed on log-amplitudes, hence absolute and relative silence floors; explicitly insensitive to phase ([ViSQOL v3](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2004.09584), [README](https://github.com/google/visqol)). NSIM(r,d) = (2μ_r μ_d + C1)/(μ_r² + μ_d² + C1) · (σ_rd + C2)/(σ_r σ_d + C2), C1 = 0.01L, C2 = (0.03L)², L = intensity range (as reproduced in [NOMAD](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.16284)); the 2015 ViSQOL paper with window/patch details is paywalled (not verified). What it rewards: matching log-envelope level (first factor) and local time-frequency correlation of the log-gammatone patch (second factor) — an over-smoothed, correctly-levelled envelope scores well (AERO no-disc: best ViSQOL, worst MUSHRA), a correctly-textured but 8 dB under-levelled band does not. Zimtohrli reports ViSQOL-audio's mean MOS correlation at 0.48 vs its own 0.68 ([Zimtohrli](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.26133v1)). LSD–ViSQOL agreement: within a paper they rank identically for FlowHigh's Table I and AP-BWE's Table V; they disagree only when critics are removed (AERO). Differentiable variants: none using NSIM directly for BWE found. NOMAD is an NSIM-*supervised* embedding distance, differentiable, gave DEMUCS PESQ +0.03…+0.11 when used as a fine-tuning loss ([NOMAD](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.16284)); FiPA-SR subtracts a differentiable PAQM (1992 masking model) term, L_G = L_adv + L_rec + 100·L_fmap − 1·L_PAQM, ViSQOL-audio 2.82 vs AudioSR 2.72 at 8 kHz music, but no ablation of that term ([FiPA-SR](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.30594)). Zimtohrli says a differentiable version is future work.

## 5. Classical envelope + noise pieces

SBR decoder: 64-band QMF, copy-up patching, envelope adjustment on a time–frequency grid, inverse filtering (whitening), noise-floor and sinusoid addition ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectral_band_replication); Dietz 2002 primary not fetched). IGF: tile copy-up + whitening + tonal-to-noise + envelope per scale-factor band (search snippets only; Fraunhofer/AES pages refused). AMR-WB: white-noise excitation shaped by an extrapolated LPC filter above 6.4 kHz (search snippet only). n-SBG replaces the DSP pipeline with a learned RVQ code and gets ViSQOL 4.38 conditional vs 3.38 blind ([n-SBG](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.06732)), i.e. blind envelope estimation, not synthesis, is the hard part.

## Ranked interventions (cheapest first, for an 88K model, incoherent band, key metrics LSD + ViSQOL-audio + CRPS)

1. **Post-hoc per-ERB-band gain on the generated high band** (zero parameters, fit on validation). Source: SBR envelope adjustment; LACE L_env. A −7.9 dB bias in 75 % of the bins alone contributes ≈0.6 to LSD (log10 power units), so most of your 0.93 is level, not texture. Prediction: LSD drops ≥0.15 on the in-distribution set, ViSQOL +0.3; on the +0.6 dB set the change is <0.02, showing the bias is input-dependent and needs (2).
2. **Add L_env (ERB-smoothed log-magnitude L1) and raise the log-magnitude weight from 0.01 to O(1–10)** inside the energy score's d(·,·). Source: LACE/Opus BWE weights, BAE-Net, n-SBG λ_mel=15. Prediction: high-band energy gap shrinks to within ±1 dB on both sets, LSD −0.05 to −0.10, CRPS unchanged or better (the score stays proper); if CRPS worsens the waveform term was doing the calibration.
3. **Linkwitz-Riley polynomial crossover instead of a hard passthrough** ([Vocos-BWE](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.07285): 0.897 → 0.850 vs naive 0.865). Prediction: LSD −0.01 to −0.03, no CRPS change.
4. **2D-conv log-magnitude discriminator (MRAD-style) at training time only** ([AP-BWE](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.06387): 0.88 → 0.84; Opus BWE). Prediction: LSD −0.04, ViSQOL +0.1; energy-score term kept so single-draw PIT stays flat; if you use a waveform MSD instead, expect AERO's LSD +0.06 regression.
5. **Folded or rectified baseband as an extra input channel next to the 8 noise channels** ([Opus BWE](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.11392) AdaShape/x·sin(log|x|); [1602.08215](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1602.08215)). The network then predicts envelope and a noise/fold mix rather than texture from scratch. Prediction: LSD-HF −0.05, ViSQOL +0.1; coherent fraction stays ≤2 %; no metallic penalty because the band is incoherent.
6. **SBR/IGF-style noise-floor mixing head**: per band, output = g_tonal·folded + g_noise·noise, both gains predicted from the baseband ERB envelope (Opus BWE's 32-band ERB conditioning). Prediction: fricative frames gain most; ViSQOL +0.1 on top of (5); fails if the model already learned this implicitly (gains saturate at 0/1).
7. **Differentiable NSIM (gammatone-log-SSIM) or the FSC-Net λ·LSD term as an auxiliary loss** ([FSC-Net](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.06962); NSIM formula above). Unverified for BWE. Prediction: ViSQOL +0.1–0.2 with LSD flat; if ViSQOL rises while MUSHRA/CRPS fall you are gaming the first NSIM factor (level) with an over-smoothed envelope, the AERO failure mode.
8. **Snake + 2× anti-aliased nonlinearity in the implicit decoder** ([BigVGAN](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2206.04658); [AERO Tab. 4](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2211.12232)). Prediction: ≤0.02 LSD, ≤0.03 ViSQOL; only worth it if aliasing above 20 kHz is visible in the draws.
